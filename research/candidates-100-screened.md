# 100 New Candidates, Screened

100 fresh trading-signal candidates, each bred to follow the DNA of the [documented winners](success-case-library.md) — genuine processing moat, a real time lead, a liquid instrument, and an explicit dollars-vs-tick cost argument — then run through the hardened 7-filter framework. Companion: [edge-hunt-conclusion.md](edge-hunt-conclusion.md).

**Result: 1 of 100 advanced past triage; 0 earned capital.** The dominant killers were Filter 2 (no informational lead — the answer is already published), Filter 1 (the data physically cannot be obtained at the needed cadence/resolution), and Filter 7 (the market is commoditized — a vendor already sells it).

## The 7-filter framework
1. **Data reality** — does the source exist and is it obtainable at the claimed cadence?
2. **Informational lead** — or does a newswire / official release beat you to it?
3. **The dollar test** — expected move in *dollars* vs the $0.01 tick and realistic spread.
4. **Cost stack** — spread + commissions + borrow + slippage + IV.
5. **Not-a-known-premium** — or is it a risk premium already harvested?
6. **Portfolio mean, not pooled median** — with de-overlapped, independent observations.
7. **Executable + capacity** — liquid, tradeable, not already crowded.
